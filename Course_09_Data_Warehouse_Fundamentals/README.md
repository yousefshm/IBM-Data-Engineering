# Data Warehouse Fundamentals

This module introduces the **core principles of data warehousing**, focusing on how analytical data systems are designed, modeled, and optimized for decision-making.

Unlike transactional databases, data warehouses are built to support **analytics, reporting, and business intelligence at scale**.

---

## 🧠 Key Concepts

- Purpose and characteristics of data warehouses
- OLTP vs OLAP systems
- Analytical workloads and reporting use cases
- Separation of operational and analytical data

The course emphasizes *why* data warehouses exist, not just *how* to build them.

---

## 🏗 Data Warehouse Architecture

Core architectural components include:

- Data sources (operational systems)
- ETL / ELT pipelines
- Staging areas
- Data warehouse layers
- Data marts

These layers enable scalable, reliable analytical querying.

---

## 🧩 Dimensional Data Modeling

A major focus of the course is **dimensional modeling**, including:

- Fact tables and dimension tables
- Star schema design
- Snowflake schema design
- Grain definition and consistency

Dimensional models are designed to make **analytics fast, intuitive, and business-friendly**.

---

## 📊 Facts and Dimensions

Key modeling concepts covered:

- Measures and metrics
- Additive, semi-additive, and non-additive facts
- Slowly Changing Dimensions (SCD)
- Time dimensions and hierarchies

These concepts ensure historical accuracy and analytical flexibility.

---

## ⚙ Performance and Optimization Considerations

The course introduces high-level optimization ideas such as:

- Query performance in analytical systems
- Indexing awareness
- Aggregations and summaries
- Schema design impact on performance

The emphasis is on **design decisions that affect long-term performance**.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

After completing this course, the learner can:

- Explain the role of data warehouses in analytics platforms
- Differentiate OLTP and OLAP systems
- Design basic dimensional models
- Choose appropriate schema types for business use cases
- Understand how warehouses support BI and reporting tools

---

## 🔗 Relevance to Data Engineering

For data engineers, data warehousing is essential because it:

- Defines how transformed data is consumed
- Influences ETL and pipeline design
- Enables scalable analytics and BI
- Bridges raw data and business insights

This course develops **architectural thinking**, not just technical execution.

---
