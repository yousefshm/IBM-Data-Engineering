# BI Dashboards with IBM Cognos Analytics and Google Looker

This module focuses on transforming curated data into **clear, interactive, and actionable business intelligence dashboards**.

It highlights how data engineering outputs are consumed by business users through modern BI and visualization tools.

---

## 🧠 Core Objectives

- Understand the role of BI tools in data platforms
- Translate business requirements into analytical dashboards
- Design effective visualizations for decision-making
- Connect data warehouses to BI layers

This course emphasizes **data storytelling**, not just charts.

---

## 📊 Business Intelligence Fundamentals

Key BI concepts covered include:

- KPIs and business metrics
- Dashboards vs reports
- Exploratory vs operational analytics
- Self-service analytics principles

The focus is on enabling **non-technical users** to interact with data.

---

## 🛠 Tools and Platforms

### 📈 IBM Cognos Analytics
- Creating dashboards and reports
- Data modeling for analytics
- Filters, drill-downs, and interactivity
- Automated insights and summaries

### 🔎 Google Looker
- Semantic modeling concepts
- Connecting to data sources
- Interactive dashboards
- Controlled self-service analytics

Both tools demonstrate **enterprise-grade BI workflows**.

---

## 🎨 Dashboard Design Principles

The course introduces best practices such as:

- Choosing the right visualization for each metric
- Avoiding misleading or cluttered charts
- Designing for clarity and usability
- Aligning visuals with business questions

Good dashboards answer questions **before they are asked**.

---

## 🔄 From Data Warehouse to Dashboard

This module reinforces the full data flow:

- ETL pipelines prepare clean data
- Data warehouses structure analytical models
- BI tools consume curated datasets
- Stakeholders make data-driven decisions

BI is presented as the **final mile** of data engineering.

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

After completing this course, the learner can:

- Build interactive BI dashboards
- Translate business needs into analytical views
- Connect BI tools to data warehouses
- Apply visualization best practices
- Support data-driven decision-making

---

## 🔗 Relevance to Data Engineering

For data engineers, BI knowledge is important to:

- Design warehouses optimized for analytics
- Anticipate how data will be visualized
- Collaborate effectively with analysts and stakeholders
- Ensure pipelines deliver business-ready data

Understanding BI closes the gap between **engineering and impact**.
