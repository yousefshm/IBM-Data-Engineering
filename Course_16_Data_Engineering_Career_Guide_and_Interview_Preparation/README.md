# Data Engineering Career Guide and Interview Preparation

This module focuses on **career planning, resume building, and interview preparation** for aspiring data engineers.

It provides actionable guidance to **showcase skills, navigate job opportunities, and succeed in technical interviews**.

---

## 🧠 Core Topics

- Overview of the data engineering job market
- Skills and competencies required for entry-level roles
- Resume and LinkedIn optimization for data engineering
- Interview strategies and common technical questions
- Soft skills and professional development

---

## 🛠 Practical Tips

- Tailor your resume to highlight:
  - Python, SQL, ETL pipelines
  - Big data tools (Spark, Hadoop)
  - Data modeling and databases
  - Workflow orchestration (Airflow, Shell scripts)
- Prepare a portfolio with:
  - Projects and capstone implementations
  - GitHub repositories with structured notes and scripts
- Practice mock interviews:
  - SQL, Python, and data pipeline design questions
  - Scenario-based questions on data engineering workflows

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

By completing this course, the learner can:

- Understand the expectations for data engineering roles
- Build a professional resume and LinkedIn profile
- Present technical skills and projects effectively
- Approach interviews with confidence
- Develop a roadmap for continuous learning and career growth

---

## 🔗 Relevance to Data Engineering

- Bridges the gap between **skills acquisition** and **job readiness**
- Highlights the importance of **portfolio projects** and practical experience
- Provides guidance for **navigating the competitive data engineering job market**
- Ensures the learner can **communicate technical expertise clearly** to recruiters and hiring managers
